# Kiwi art — prompts for Ellie's logo (Dulce Kiwi)

Generate in ChatGPT (image) or Gemini. Make a FEW, drop the PNGs in this folder.
Then tell Claude "the kiwis are in" and he'll build the stamp around them.

## What we need (important)
- Just the KIWI BIRD. No text, no circle, no plate, no background scene.
- SOLID flat silhouette, ONE colour, no shading/gradient/3D.
- Pure WHITE background (or transparent). Nothing else in the frame.
- Kiwi facing RIGHT, standing, full body: round fluffy body, long slender
  slightly down-curved beak, two little legs/feet.
- Friendly, simple, characterful — like a hand-carved rubber stamp.
- Square image, kiwi centred with generous empty margin around it.
- Highest resolution available.

---

## PROMPT 1 — clean silhouette (best for stamping)
A minimalist solid black silhouette of a kiwi bird, facing right, standing on
its two small legs, with a round fluffy body and a long slender slightly
down-curved beak. Flat single-colour silhouette, no shading, no outline, no
texture, no gradient. Pure white background, nothing else. Centred with wide
margins. Simple, friendly, like a hand-carved rubber stamp icon. Square image.

## PROMPT 2 — linocut / hand-stamp texture
A kiwi bird in a rustic linocut / hand-carved rubber-stamp style, facing right,
round body, long curved beak, standing on two legs. Solid dark ink with subtle
rough pressed-ink edges, single colour, on a plain white background. No text, no
frame, no circle. Centred, lots of margin. Earthy, artisanal, handmade feel.

## PROMPT 3 — slightly more detailed but still flat
A charming flat illustration of a kiwi bird for a bakery logo, facing right,
plump rounded body, long thin down-curved beak, tiny legs. Single dark-green
colour, flat, no shading, clean simple shapes, white background, no text, no
border. Centred, square, generous margins.

## Tips
- If it adds a circle/text/background anyway, say: "remove all text and the
  circle, just the bird alone on pure white."
- Black or dark green both fine — Claude will recolour to the brand green.
- Transparent PNG is ideal; flat white also works.
- 3–6 options is plenty; we'll pick the best body + best beak and refine.
